What still ties Android to Google Play services?

microG is the open-source replacement for Google Play services. This map shows which Play services features it already covers, which are missing, and how many people are waiting for each one.

Why now: Google plans to require developer registration for all apps from 2027. According to Keep Android Open, the installation of unregistered apps is controlled through Google Play services, not Android itself. Phones running a de-Googled Android with microG are outside that. Every gap closed here makes that switch easier.

How to help

  • Pick a missing tile, open its issue and read what is already known.
  • Reverse-engineer the client side and Play services side, write it down, and send focused pull requests with proof from a real device. The Cast & DIAL notes show the method.
  • Test open pull requests on your own hardware and report results.

What code can't fix

Apps that demand Play Integrity's device or strong verdict (many banks, Google Wallet) only accept Google-certified, locked phones. microG can't pass that honestly. That part needs pressure on app makers and regulators.
